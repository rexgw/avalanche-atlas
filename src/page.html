            Identify a cone

            Upload a photo of a pinecone to compare it with the thirteen cone types in the atlas. You get the likelihood of each one, and the features that decided it.

            • Shoot from the side for shape and length, in even light such as open shade or an overcast sky.
            • Add a second photo from the tip end to show the scale tips, prickles and symmetry.
            • Include a coin, pen or hand for size. Without one, a small cone and a big one can look alike.
            • Needles per bundle are the most telling clue for pines. Photograph a fallen bundle or choose the count above.

            The steps follow the Fieldbook pine cone identification guide.

            Photos are sent for analysis on your own account and are not stored by this page.
